### Architecture & Training

- [Bongard: Training Machine Intuition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39111) — Trains an open System One model with outcome feedback. `Method` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.39111v1)
- [OpenJev-RLCD: A Working RLCD Implementation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38850) — Implements a third-party calibrated-decision reinforcement objective. `Method` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.38850v1) · [Code](https://github.com/ZimmyGao/openjev-rlcd)
- [Chinese-Jev: Bringing System One Model to Chinese-Language Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36965) — Develops a System One model for Chinese-language tasks. `Method` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.36965v1)
- [LAVOIR: Teaching a Single-Pass Decision Encoder When and What to Ask with Amortized Value of Information](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30706) — Learns which missing information is worth asking for. `Method` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.30706v1)
- [Universal Fractal Natural Language Decision Map: Real-Time Edge Triage Across Heterogeneous Domains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25498) — Reports an alternative edge decision implementation and benchmark comparison. `Method` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.25498v2)
- [this-that-model-1.0: A typed decision model that decides in 30 ms, for a millionth of a cent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23886) — Provides an open model with a native typed decision interface. `Method` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.23886v1)
- [Dyad: Extending Large Language Models with Native Typed Decision-Making](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36116) — Adds an action-description encoder to an LLM and evaluates frozen-backbone and joint reinforcement learning for typed decisions. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.36116v1)
- [OmniMed-Jev: Calibrating LVLM Confidence for Trustworthy Medical Multimodal Decisions via System One](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00381) — Studies candidate-conditioned multimodal decisions against a matched generative baseline, reporting improved calibration with task-dependent accuracy trade-offs. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.00381v1)
- [Tron-1B: Fast, Calibrated Typed Decisions with a Set-Attention Option Head](https://zenodo.org/records/23066522) — Introduces a bidirectional encoder with a set-attention option head; benchmark-trained results are compared with a zero-shot hosted service. `Model` · [PDF](https://zenodo.org/api/records/23066522/files/tron-1b-paper.pdf/content)
- [ufakzeka-karar: An Open Turkish Typed-Decision Model with Order-Invariant Option Scoring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06744) — Scores Turkish answer options independently at shared positions to remove order effects; supervised training beats the tested REINFORCE variant, while temperature calibration degrades on held-out question types. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.06744v1)
- [SanSi: A Looped Typed Decision Model for System 1.5 Thinking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07730) — Trains typed readouts after repeated backbone loops; eight loops improve accuracy at fixed model shape, but cost more compute and calibrate worse than three loops. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.07730v1)
- [Visual Jev Rewards: Reference-Bound Verification for Multi-Subject Image Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09328) — Trains a local Qwen verifier for joint reference identity and image conditions, then averages binary probabilities as an image-generation reward. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.09328v1)
- [MetaEncoder: Exploring the Limit of Bi-Encoders for Multimodal System One Decision Making with Natural Language Interface](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11316) — Trains a multimodal bi-encoder with request-to-candidate contrastive learning; including options in requests improves closed-set decisions while retaining cached candidate embeddings. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.11316v1)
- [VideoJev: System One Model for Video Understanding and Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18615) — Uses shared decision heads and reusable visual computation for temporal grounding and video QA; the authors report faster inference with comparable or improved task scores. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18615v1)
- [Proactive Physical AI through Continuous Observation and Timely Intervention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16967) — Introduces a Jev-style timing model that separates intervention decisions from response generation, with video-derived supervision for continuous physical-world observation. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.16967v1)
- [Jeva: System One Models from First Principles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18773) — Introduces a decision architecture with independent option encoding, symmetric aggregation and separate coverage estimation to support permutation invariance, null outcomes and cached reuse. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.18773v1)
- [StartLux-Decision: AutoThink Decision Models with Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Deliberative Decisions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17309) — Combines single-pass Instant models with confidence-guided AutoThink reasoning; calibrated deliberative reinforcement learning reduces reasoning length while preserving reported accuracy. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.17309v1)
- [Releasing the Inner Jev: A Simple Recipe for Open Decision Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17205) — Reads option-letter logits from instruction-tuned LMs and improves them with LoRA decision-head tuning; evaluates accuracy and calibration on OpenDecisions and JevBench. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.17205v1)
- [FunDM: Cheap & Label-Free Decision Models from Frozen Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17175) — Distills frozen language-model probabilities into small decision heads without correctness labels, retaining most multiple-choice accuracy with fewer transformer layers. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.17175v1)
- [Intern-Decision: Efficient Multimodal Decision Making with Autoregressive Masked Language Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22131) — Uses masked decision placeholders in a JSON skeleton for one-pass multimodal choices; reports compact-model accuracy, latency and small calibration and handoff pilots. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.22131v1)
- [Qwen-Audio-Jev: A Unified Interface for Audio Decisions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21614) — Adds typed CHOICE, NOUL and SCORE readouts to an audio backbone; reports results across 18 labeled configurations and about 20-fold inference speedup over its generative backbone. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.21614v1)
- [Verdict: Encode Once, Decide Many](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20778) — Shares a question-independent state encoding across typed questions with option invariance; improves agreement with soft labels but exposes accuracy–latency costs of removing question-aware state encoding. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.20778v1)
- [Chinese Decision: A Family of Language Models for Single-Pass Probabilistic Decision-Making](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23170) — Trains Chinese single-pass decision models using candidate-letter logits and distribution targets; the completed 0.8B model reports 1.07% calibration error, while partial 2B training does not isolate a scale effect. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.23170v1) · [Code](https://github.com/yanqiangmiffy/chinese-decision)
- [TimesJev: Thinking Fast on Time Series with System 1 Sufficiency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26002) — Learns gated time-series compression and embedding alignment from sufficiency and realizability objectives, enabling single-pass decisions; reports improved performance and lower latency across 13 datasets. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.26002v2)
- [Narrow Decision Models: A System-1 Architecture for Calibrated, Typed Decisions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25553) — Uses prefix-tree block attention and logical positions to make organization-specific decisions invariant to option order and independent across questions; small-scale validation tests these properties and routing behavior. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.25553v1)
- [System X: An LLM Decision Model That Replies from Its Own KV Cache](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25456) — Adds a text-reply choice and reuses the decision KV cache for generation; measured latency falls, but support-task gains trade off against instruction following and reply preferences depend on the judge. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.25456v1)
- [UniJev: Separating Accuracy from Calibration in Trimodal System One Decision Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25784) — Separates supervised accuracy gains from calibration in text, image and video decision models; confidence-outcome alignment lowers visual ECE at 2B and 9B while preserving accuracy without reinforcement learning. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.25784v1)
- [Our Own Jev: Typed Decisions in One Forward Pass, from an In-House Judge to Open LoRA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29052) — Reports an internal typed judge and open LoRA decision models. Public hard-tier results are single-seed and developed with the evaluation items in view; corpus revisions shift small-benchmark accuracy substantially. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.29052v1)
- [RubriX: A Fast, Fine-Tunable Non-Autoregressive Rubric Judge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34875) — Uses separate encoder representations and cached rubric-level embeddings for non-generative judging. It improves held-out rubric accuracy and keeps latency nearly constant with long level descriptions; human-labelled benchmarks and ranking show mixed advantages over Jev. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.34875v1)
- [Seeing the Options: Typed Decisions over Visual Candidates with Reusable Option Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33702) — Extends typed decisions to visual candidates with reusable option encodings. Request-conditioned independent branches retain candidate-order invariance and improve accuracy over independent encoding, while caching reduces repeated-candidate inference cost. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.33702v1)
- [Decision-Native Recommendation with Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34124) — Uses tree-structured attention to share user-history computation across isolated candidate branches. The decision interface tests order-consistent choices, calibrated confidence and selective computation in sequential recommendation. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.34124v1)
- [Improving JEV Entailment Reranking with Span Readouts and Selective Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35654) — Combines mean-span option readouts with selective attention and Gated DeltaNet adapters for cross-request entailment reranking. On MC11, the authors report gains for NeoHorse-1-4B while more than halving trainable parameters; direct-decision transfer is not established by the current abstract. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35654v2)
- [PsyJev: Psychological Analysis via System-1 Judgment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36689) — Adapts RLCD to heterogeneous question-conditioned judgments through balanced supervision, retention of correct supervised predictions and question-group rewards. Controlled small-model comparisons examine training stages and probability quality on psychological-analysis tasks. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.36689v1)
- [Audio System One Model: Typed Probabilistic Perception of Emotion in Speech](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22401) — Introduces ASOM, a 6.2M-parameter reader over frozen speech and text encoders that answers typed questions in one non-generative pass. Trained on listener-vote distributions, it evaluates new label sets and transfer across speech corpora; reported results await detailed full-text audit. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.22401v1)
- [Flide: Accelerating Decision Inference with Exact State Forking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42148) — Introduces exact state forking across attention and recurrent Gated DeltaNet layers, separating logical decision branches from flattened execution. The authors report up to 9.5× faster queries than vLLM/SGLang; exact-arithmetic equivalence and measured floating-point deviations are distinct claims. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.42148v1)
- [Zuse: A Decision Model for European Languages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06238) — Evaluates commercial Zuse adapters on 20,044 European development rows and 32,700 multilingual rows. Zuse-27B reports 87.5% development accuracy, pooled ECE 0.016 versus source-weighted 0.035, and threshold-transfer failures; weights are not openly released, and selection reuse limits generalization claims. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.06238v1) · [Code](https://github.com/binericai/models/tree/main/zuse)
- [Towards Foundation Decision Models: Drex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07854) — Presents four Drex typed decision models with supervised training, adjusted-feedback reinforcement learning and option-consistency losses. Authors report Max scoring 67.52 versus Jev 1.14.0 at 60.70 on Decision Index 0.4.1; model artifacts and detailed benchmark comparability remain to be audited. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.07854v1) · [Code](https://github.com/nace-ai/drex-decision-models)
- [Extending Reasoning in Typed Decision Models via Looping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07736) — RELOAD jointly trains damped middle-layer loops with a typed decision model and learns an input-adaptive pass controller. Across six 0.4B–4B models, authors report improved accuracy and calibration, especially beyond training reasoning depth; the controller retains most gains at roughly three quarters of the compute. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.07736v1)

### Decision Interfaces

- [LLM2Jev: LLMs Are Already Jev-Style Decision Models -- When and How to Fine-Tune Them](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02076) — Studies when existing LLMs already support Jev-style decisions. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.02076v1)
- [Benchmarking System One decision models against trained classifiers and language models for automated decision gates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00346) — Compares typed models, trained classifiers and LLM readouts under matched requests. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.00346v1)
- [Koa-action: Fast and Consistent Structured Decision Making with Generative LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36115) — Uses atomic output tokens for fast structured decisions. `Method` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.36115v1)
- [From Text Decisions to Pixels: An Study of Jev-Style Visual Choice Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29283) — Studies visual choice readouts with matched generation baselines. `Method` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.29283v1)
- [Jev in Practice: A Composable Python Toolkit for TypeSafe’s System One Decision Model](https://zenodo.org/records/22921974) — Provides composable tooling, calibration utilities and recorded API experiments. `Tool` · [PDF](https://zenodo.org/api/records/22921974/files/daf-jev_combined.pdf/content)
- [NumericJev: Jev-like LLM Numerical Decoding with Multiway Decision Trees](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28587) — Uses multiway decision trees for numerical readout. `Method` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.28587v1)
- [AnyJev Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00831) — Reads option probabilities from pretrained LLMs and corrects label and position bias with prior normalization and cyclic option rotations. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.00831v1)
- [System One Models for Wireless Decision-Making:Applications and Performance Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04345) — Measures the quality/latency trade-off in antenna selection and RAN slicing: Jev responds faster, while utility gains depend on the task and execution costs. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.04345v1)
- [Jarvis: Coordination Deserves a Brain of Its Own in Agentic Interactive Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23804) — Coordinates live tasks with typed decisions and version checks, revalidating plans and spoken results when requests change; matched front/back models improve voice-task success from 29 to 192 of 278 tasks. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.23804v1)
- [P(Yes): 15$\times$ Improvements via Test-Time Scaling of Decision Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25474) — Searches candidate answers using frozen models' Yes probabilities, improving logic-puzzle accuracy without training; the headline 15-fold gain is exact correctness from 2 to 30 of 128 puzzles. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.25474v1)
- [SOMA: Continuous Monitoring and Intervention for System Two Reasoning with System One Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26793) — Continuously monitors reasoning with a shared decision model and escalating interventions; reports up to 36% fewer reasoning tokens and higher abstention on unanswerable questions without training. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.26793v1)
- [Multimodal Reranking Is a System One Decision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28435) — Treats multimodal reranking as a single choice over retrieved candidates; choice readouts outperform per-candidate yes/no scoring, while text-only decision tuning improves document reranking. Cross-model comparisons are confounded by size and generation. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.28435v1) · [Code](https://github.com/Ryenhails/rerank-as-decision)
- [S1CogBench: What System One Decision Models Can and Cannot Do on Cognitive Experiments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00911) — Evaluates five open models and Jev on 24 cognitive tasks, separating task conditions, scoring and disclosed adaptations. It studies whether decisions respond to evidence, feedback, memory demand and future opportunities; aggregate accuracy and human-behavior prediction are distinct outcomes. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.00911v1)
- [To Jev or Not to Jev: When Can System-1 Decision Models Replace LLM Judges?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00505) — Compares 14 decision models and four generative judge configurations across eleven judge benchmarks, including reference-guided evaluation, confidence cascades and injected instructions. Some models approach generative judges on tested settings, but injection susceptibility varies sharply by model; reference-generation costs and statistical comparisons need full-text audit. `Evaluation` · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.00505v1)
- [Probabilistic Relevance Decisions for Semantic Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08014) — Compares BGE-M3 retrieval with Jev Noul and OpenAI Predicate reranking on 300 SciFact test queries.
